### shaan-audience-mailbag — How would you build an audience from scratch in 2024?

[Shaan Puri](https://www.shaanpuri.com/mailbag/how-would-you-build-an-audience-from-scratch-in-2024) · Host-authored public advice article

Locator: Lessons 1–9; especially 1–4, 6, 8, 9

Prioritize the particular people your work attracts over raw follower totals. Write about subjects you naturally obsess over; reveal experiences, interests, ambitions, and what you offer readers to build connection. Decide which feeling your content should consistently deliver. Strong substance matters more than polished delivery; length is less important than sustaining interest. Improve one component with each new piece rather than waiting for instant audience growth.

Limits: Personal experience and creator advice; no calibrated engagement prediction. The 5 Ds concern a body of work, not mandatory elements of every post.

### sam-capitalism-transcript — Sam Parr — How He Disrupted Media & And Got An 8-Figure Exit — Transcript

[Sam Parr, interviewed by Ryan Moran](https://www.capitalism.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Sam-Parr-How-He-Disrupted-Media-And-Got-An-8-Figure-Exit-Transcript.pdf) · Publisher-hosted public interview transcript, 33 pages

Locator: 00:14:11–00:17:21, PDF pages 6–7; 00:18:50–00:23:25, pages 8–10; 00:34:56–00:35:40, pages 16–17

Identify the intended audience and where it will discover the piece; connect distribution, headline, story, and intended emotion. Study popular conversations and comments in communities your readers use, then contribute a distinctive perspective or useful detail. Understand the motivation behind the reader's desired action. Hand-copy strong work to discover patterns before creating your own writing.

Limits: Transcript is publicly readable but contains transcription errors. Sam expressly distinguishes this approach from SEO-first content. Publication date is not stated; URL contains 2021/12.
