# Final rewrite checks

Use the pinned [No AI Slop rules](no-ai-slop/SKILL.md) and [evaluation](no-ai-slop/eval.md), by Peter Yang, alongside the MFM principles. The original [MIT license](no-ai-slop/LICENSE) is included.

## Before returning a rewrite

Preserve the original, intended meaning, supplied facts, language, audience, constraints, and voice traits. Record specific original patterns and the problems they cause. A word match is a candidate finding, not evidence of AI authorship.

Make proportionate edits, then run a distinct audit of the exact candidate and any proposed line replacements:

| Check | Pass evidence |
|---|---|
| Meaning | The author's point, reasoning, and meaningful uncertainty remain intact. |
| Factual fidelity | Details match the supplied material or checked research; no invented results or attribution. |
| Voice | The text retains the author's recognizable perspective and register. |
| Proportionate editing | Changes fix identified weaknesses without stripping useful depth or personality. |
| Clarity | Titles identify a specific subject or useful promise; each point has one clear formulation without needless restatement; the opening, argument, paragraph order, and sentence handoffs work for the reader. |
| No AI Slop | The pinned rules and evaluation have been applied to the candidate. |
| User constraints | The requested format, scope, language, and instructions are honored. |

Record Pass or Fail with concrete evidence for each. A desired numerical score is never evidence.

For founder outreach, apply the [outreach checks](outreach.md#review-checks) under the existing voice, factual-fidelity, and user-constraint checks. Keep this guidance specific to outreach; it does not require a sales hook or CTA in other writing.

For the title and concision requirements in [structure and flow](structure-flow.md), inspect every heading and compare ideas across the entire piece. Generic titles and repeated claims disguised with different words fail the existing clarity and user-constraint checks. Repair them by replacing or removing the vague heading and merging duplicate ideas while preserving distinct facts, evidence, and caveats. Do not merely flag the problem and return the same text, or withhold a rewrite when a supported repair is available. Optional word choices, repetition of a necessary term, and genuinely useful explanations are not failures.

## Repair instead of abandoning the rewrite

Fix failed checks and audit the new text again. Attempt up to two targeted repairs. If useful work remains, build a fresh, simpler version from the supplied material and the failure findings, omit optional line replacements, and check it again.

If it still fails, return the original assessment and the concrete reason the rewrite could not be released. Identify any specific missing information. Do not silently bypass the failed check, drop the finding, or pretend an unavailable audit passed.

Use a separate reviewer/model call when available and authorized. Otherwise use a distinct self-review pass and disclose that method. Separate review is not a prerequisite for running this skill in chat.

## What must not become a new gate

- An uncommon word, retained technical term, sentence length, or unknown reading grade.
- A score below 92, a small score gain, or an unavailable scoring pass.
- A missing sales CTA, joke, personal story, or emotional reveal when the format does not call for it.
- Research being unavailable, provided unresolved claims remain qualified or omitted and the limitation is visible.

Plain-language preferences belong in the edits. They do not independently block a rewrite or trigger an automatic score deduction.

## Research and corrections

Checked public evidence is authorized factual material alongside the original draft. A supported correction can differ from the original without violating fidelity. Give the auditor the evidence and preserve its exact scope; do not restore a contradicted original claim.

Fidelity alone does not establish external truth. Distinguish confirmed facts, supported corrections, and unresolved claims. Never label an unverified central claim as publication-ready.

## Final reporting

Return the checked rewrite, its score when available, and a concise explanation connecting the main changes to the original weaknesses. State the audit method honestly. These are editorial checks, not guarantees of truth or future performance.
